1. Overview

SignalLedger ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the Platform.

This Platform is designed with a privacy-first architecture: it does not require user registration, does not store personally identifiable information on our servers, and does not sell or share personal data with third-party advertisers.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

We do not require you to create an account. When using the Platform, you may input:

  • Stock ticker symbols for analysis (e.g., "AAPL", "MSFT"). These are sent to our backend API solely to retrieve public market data.
  • CSV files (optional) containing historical price data you upload for local analysis. CSV data is processed client-side or temporarily in memory and is not stored on our servers beyond the immediate request lifecycle.

2.2 Automatically Collected Technical Data

Our backend server may log the following for operational and security purposes:

  • Your IP address (pseudonymised in server logs after 24 hours).
  • HTTP request method, path, and response status code.
  • Browser User-Agent string.
  • Timestamp and request duration.

These logs are used exclusively for debugging, security monitoring, and performance optimisation. They are not used for profiling or targeted advertising.

3. Local Storage & Session Data

The Platform uses your browser's Local Storage to persist certain preferences locally on your device, without transmitting them to our servers:

  • Theme preference — your selected dark/light mode (key: stock_ledger_theme).
  • Search history — recent ticker lookups stored locally (key: stock_ledger_history).
  • Watchlist — tickers you've starred or added to your watchlist (key: stock_ledger_watchlist).

This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's local storage or site data for this domain.

4. Third-Party Data Sources

Market data is retrieved from Yahoo Finance public data endpoints. When you request data for a ticker, our server makes an outbound request to Yahoo Finance's APIs on your behalf. Please be aware:

  • Yahoo Finance's own Privacy Policy governs their data collection from those requests.
  • Ticker symbols you analyse are included in the query parameters of requests to Yahoo Finance's servers.
  • We implement server-side caching to minimise the number of outbound requests to third-party APIs.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party advertising or tracking SDK.

5. Cookies & Tracking

SignalLedger does not set first-party cookies. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or fingerprinting technologies.

Your browser may set cookies as part of standard TLS session management or CORS preflight handling, but these are functional and ephemeral — they are not used to track your activity across sessions or sites.

6. How We Use Information

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Retrieve and process public market data in response to your requests.
  • Maintain session performance through server-side caching (data is cached by ticker symbol, not by user).
  • Monitor and improve Platform performance, reliability, and security.
  • Investigate and resolve technical issues and security incidents.

We do not use your data to build profiles, serve personalised advertisements, or sell insights to third parties.

7. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may disclose information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Legal compliance: If required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Security: To investigate, prevent, or take action against actual or suspected fraud, abuse, or illegal activity.
  • Business continuity: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, where the receiving party agrees to maintain the same privacy standards.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed by the Platform, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all client-server communications.
  • Parameterised API requests to prevent injection attacks.
  • Input sanitisation and validation on all user-supplied parameters.
  • Server-side rate limiting to prevent denial-of-service abuse.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you not to submit sensitive personal or financial information through this Platform beyond what is required to use its features.

9. Your Rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data beyond server logs (which are pseudonymised and automatically deleted), most formal data subject rights (access, erasure, portability) do not practically apply.

You retain full control over locally stored data (history, watchlist, theme) and can delete it at any time through your browser's developer tools or privacy settings. Clearing site data for this domain removes all locally stored preferences.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected personal information, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

10. Children's Privacy

SignalLedger is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the Platform after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please raise an issue in the project's official code repository. We aim to respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.

For matters involving sensitive personal data requests, we recommend contacting a qualified legal professional in your jurisdiction.
